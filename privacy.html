Privacy policy

Idle Heartwood, the Heartwood TCG and the UrgerBae Card Club · Last updated 3 November 2026

These games are run by Sydney's Finest Meats Pty Ltd (ABN 19 681 732 776), trading as UrgerBae and Sydney's Finest Meats, at 12 Carwar Ave, Carss Park NSW 2221 ("we"). This page explains what we collect, why, and how to have it deleted. In short: we collect only what the games need, we don't run ads, analytics or tracking, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

When you play

  • An account ID. The first time you open a game it creates an anonymous account with a random ID, so your progress can be saved online. It doesn't contain your name.
  • Your game progress: your save, cards, decks, match results and the times you last played, so your progress loads on any device.
  • If you choose "Sign in with Google": Google gives us your email address, name and profile picture. We only use the email address, to show which account you're signed in with and to let you load your progress on another device.

When you join in with others

  • The ladder: the player name you choose, your rating, wins and losses and your deck's faction. Other players can see these, so don't use your real name.
  • Polls: which option you voted for. Vote totals are visible to other players; who voted isn't shown in the game.
  • Bug reports and ideas: what you write, plus details that help fix bugs: game version, level and progress, browser and device type, screen size and recent error messages. Reports are posted publicly on GitHub (github.com/FinestMeats/idle-heartwood) under a random player code, without your name or email, so please don't write personal details in them.

In the UrgerBae Card Club

  • Your binder (which cards you own), the dates you scanned the counter QR code, reward claim codes and the rewards you've claimed.
  • When you claim a reward, our staff see your binder, your scan dates and past claims, so they can check the claim.

What we don't do

  • No ads, analytics, session recording or tracking tools, and no tracking cookies.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information for marketing.
  • We don't ask for your age, address, phone number, photos or location.

Where it's stored and who handles it

  • Google Firebase stores accounts and game data. Google's servers may be outside Australia.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the games, and GitHub hosts bug reports. Like any website host, GitHub records the IP address of visitors.
  • The Card Club page loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when you open it.
  • Your device keeps a copy of your save in the browser's local storage, so the game works offline. Clearing your browser data removes it from that device.

We use these providers only to run the games, and they handle your information under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data while you play. If you ask us to delete it, we delete your account, saves, cards, ladder row, poll votes and Card Club binder. We keep a record of rewards already handed over, so the same reward can't be claimed twice. Bug reports already posted to GitHub can be removed on request.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages and we don't knowingly collect more from children than the game needs. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's account and data at any time.

Your choices

  • See or delete your data: email contact@finestmeats.com.au with the account ID shown in the game, or the Google account you signed in with. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Correct something: you can change your ladder name in the game at any time.
  • Complaints: contact us first. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

If something goes wrong

If we learn that game data has been accessed by someone who shouldn't have it, we'll fix the cause and tell affected players in the game and on this page as soon as we can.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect, we'll say so in the game first.
