  • Don't cheat. That includes editing saves, using scripts or bots, exploiting bugs on purpose, running several accounts to collect extra rewards, or attacking the game's servers.

UrgerBae Card Club rules

How it works

  • Open the Card Club on your phone and scan the QR code at the UrgerBae counter for a free pack of 6 cards. You get one pack a day per account, plus a welcome pack the first time. No purchase is necessary.
  • Each day's code works only on that day (Sydney time).
  • Four duplicate cards of the same rarity can be traded for a missing card of that rarity, up to Epic.

We may change the rewards, odds or rules, or end the Card Club. We'll give at least 14 days' notice in the Card Club, and you can still claim any reward for a set you finished before the change.
